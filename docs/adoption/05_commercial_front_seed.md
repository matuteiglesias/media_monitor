# Commercial-front seed

> **Status:** candidate positioning copy · **not yet a public offer, price sheet, license or warranty**

## Purpose

This page stores the smallest coherent commercial framing that AP0 can test and refine. It is intentionally evidence-calibrated.

## Candidate one-line proposition

> Deploy a governed media-intelligence workflow around your sources, watch priorities and editorial authority — from monitored evidence through deterministic prioritization and context to human-approved delivery or publication.

## Candidate short explanation

Media Monitor is a deployed, contract-driven news-intelligence and editorial publishing system. It separates monitored signals, deterministic selection, observed context, assisted editorial generation, human approval and public delivery instead of collapsing them into one opaque AI workflow.

For an adopter, the core proposition is not "use our Argentina news site." The reusable path is to provide your own source strategy or signal feed, topics/watch policy, identity and delivery requirements while retaining the same governed compiler and authority boundaries.

## What exists today

Evidence-backed current surfaces include:

- a deployed canonical Media Monitor outlet and public health endpoint;
- deterministic local demo;
- versioned contracts and CI;
- configurable selection/context/snapshot machinery;
- a second-instance fixture demonstrating that another outlet can compile without adopter-specific edits to generic core builders;
- human-gated article promotion/publication semantics;
- deployment/operation tooling for the existing instance.

## What has not yet been proven

Do not imply that the repository already has:

- a live external customer/adopter instance;
- self-service onboarding;
- generic private multi-user CMS;
- enterprise auth/SSO;
- established SLA/support commitments;
- a chosen public/commercial code license;
- generic billing/checkout;
- every external monitoring-provider connector;
- every report/notification channel.

Those are adoption-loop surfaces or future evidence, not current claims.

## Candidate engagement paths

### Bounded pilot

A small, explicitly scoped instance around one organization/use case, with agreed sources/input mode, watch policy, operator flow, deliverable and acceptance criteria.

### Managed white-label deployment

A separately branded instance using reusable Media Monitor machinery while the adopter retains its client/editorial identity and commercial relationship.

### Technology / license partnership

The adopter integrates or operates the governed compiler around its own data/source infrastructure. Exact licensing terms remain a human decision.

### Strategic transfer

A buyer evaluates the code, contracts, deployment assets, documentation and operational know-how for acquisition/technology transfer. Transfer mechanics are not yet encoded.

## What the adopter provides

At minimum:

- use case and recipient;
- sources or source-interface decision;
- topics/watch priorities;
- geography/language;
- brand/identity requirements;
- approval authority;
- public/private mode;
- desired deliverable/cadence;
- pilot acceptance criteria.

## What the adopter should receive

For a credible pilot:

- an isolated configured preview;
- provenance-preserving monitored/selected/context state;
- explicit human authority where editorial content is produced;
- the agreed recipient-facing deliverable;
- health/freshness evidence;
- a bounded acceptance/handoff report.
