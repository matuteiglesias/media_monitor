## Publication evidence classes

### Production

Only real `published_article.v1` rows in the production publication bus/snapshot count as public editorial publication.

### Rehearsal / toy acceptance

`artifacts/editorial_c3_rehearsal/` and the toy-approved acceptance tranche exist to exercise consumers and gates. They are explicitly not publication decisions.

### Adopter demo

`.demo/` output is deterministic fixture material and never live news.

## Visual evidence policy

The repository does not check in a fabricated or stale homepage screenshot merely to make the README look complete.

For visual PRs, capture screenshots from the actual changed surface and attach them to the PR. A future automated screenshot pipeline may persist stable public captures, but until then live routes + machine-readable health + browser/crawler acceptance are the stronger canonical evidence.

Article OpenGraph cards are generated by the real Next route and checked through metadata/crawler contracts; they should not be represented as proof of a public article unless that article genuinely exists in the production publication projection.
